# ICLR 2027 AI-Use Disclosure — Draft

This draft is intentionally conservative. It should be expanded rather than narrowed if later provenance review identifies additional AI-assisted research tasks.

## Draft disclosure

Generative AI tools were used as research assistants during parts of this project, including research ideation and critique, literature-search support and summarization, refinement of hypotheses and experimental methodology, software/code drafting and editing, analysis and interpretation support, repository and reproducibility checks, and drafting/editing of manuscript text. AI-assisted outputs were not treated as scientific evidence merely because they were generated by a model; claims and quantitative statements were checked against the project’s committed task records, deterministic scoring code, statistical artifacts, executable tests, and cited source material as applicable. Generative AI systems were not used as judges for the benchmark’s reported task outcomes. Human annotation used to audit the deterministic scorer is reported separately in the manuscript, including the author-annotator role and the independent annotator role. The author reviewed the final research claims and takes responsibility for the content of the submission, including any text, code, analysis, or artifacts produced with generative-AI assistance.

## ICLR policy coverage checklist

ICLR 2027 requires disclosure when generative AI was used for research tasks including hypothesis refinement, methodology/experiment design, implementation, data processing, qualitative analysis, interpretation of results, translation, theoretical/model development, mathematical claims, or proofs. It recommends disclosure for literature search/summarization, code editing, figure work, drafting/editing prose, reference formatting, paper structure, titles, keywords, brainstorming, and topic/gap discovery.

Before submission, verify the actual project history against this list and explicitly add any used category that is not already represented above.

## Non-claims

This disclosure does not claim that every AI-assisted artifact received independent human replication. It states the narrower, auditable workflow: AI assistance occurred; evidence authority remained in frozen repository artifacts and source documents; the human author retains responsibility.
